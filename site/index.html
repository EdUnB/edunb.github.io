01 /ABOUT

RESEARCH AT THE INTERSECTION OF DISCIPLINES

Science that connects processes, people and planet.

I am a professor at the Department of Mechanical Engineering, University of Brasília (UnB), and a researcher at the Energy and Environment Laboratory (LEA).

My work combines experiments and numerical methods to understand and optimize energy conversion processes, with particular emphasis on thermochemical technologies for biomass and residual resources.

I hold a dual PhD in Mechanical Sciences from the University of Brasília and Wood & Fiber Sciences from Université de Lorraine, France. My research bridges fundamental physical and physicochemical principles with energy, exergy and life-cycle performance.

I also contribute to graduate education through PCMEC and work with national and international research partners to translate scientific evidence into sustainable technologies.
